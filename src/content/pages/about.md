I've spent the last fifteen years helping people with their technology, all
of it remotely. I started in billing and account support for a
telecommunications provider, moved into technical support, and grew into
Tier 2 and white-glove support for SaaS companies, where I also trained the
agents coming up behind me.

Along the way I supported Apple customers through the AppleCare program,
helped non-profits untangle payments and financial reports at Daxko, and most
recently worked client cases as a Technical Support Engineer at One Inc:
reviewing logs, reproducing issues, and escalating real bugs to the
development and product teams.

What I enjoy most is the moment a frustrated customer realizes the problem is
solved and understands why. I like digging into logs to reproduce a tricky
bug just as much as walking someone through a fix on a Zoom call.

I'm looking for my next role in technical support, client success, or support
engineering at a company that cares about its customers. If that sounds like
your team, I'd love to talk.
