I've spent more than ten years helping people with their technology, all of
it remotely. I started in billing and account support for a
telecommunications provider, moved into technical support, and grew into
Tier 2 and white-glove support for SaaS companies, where I also trained the
agents coming up behind me.

Along the way I supported Apple customers through the AppleCare program and
helped non-profits untangle payments and financial reports at Daxko. Today
I'm a Support Engineer at One Inc, a payments software company, where I dig
through Elastic and Grafana logs, reproduce API issues in Postman, meet with
clients, and turn real bugs into clear tickets for engineering.

What I enjoy most is the moment a frustrated customer realizes the problem is
solved and understands why. I like digging into logs to reproduce a tricky
bug just as much as walking someone through a fix on a Zoom call.

I'm always glad to talk about roles in technical support, client success, or
support engineering at companies that care about their customers.
